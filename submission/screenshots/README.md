# Paper figure screenshot crops

These files are deterministic crops of the recorded VeriNICE interface. They
contain no regenerated UI content and may be assembled as independent panels:

- `panel-main-interface.png`: full workbench context.
- `panel-decomposed-claims.png`: atomic-claim selection panel.
- `panel-evidence-source.png`: source-document panel.
- `panel-reasoning-graph.png`: reasoning graph panel.

Keep the crops at their native aspect ratios when placing them in the paper.
